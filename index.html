About Simone

SY

I'm a senior at UCLA studying Statistics & Data Science, with a minor in Film, TV, and Digital Media. My start was in storytelling — a foundation that taught me how to understand an audience, communicate with intention, and turn a complicated problem into an experience that resonates.

What excites me most is turning an idea into something people can feel, use, and return to. I've analyzed ambassador campaigns, led user research for advisory clients, built product roadmaps, and consulted on strategy for companies from Reddit to Uber — and I'm now expanding that foundation into product management, leading cross-functional teams and building my own products on the side.

"Everything that happens starts from the mind."

Based in Los Angeles, California. Taiwanese American. Outside of product, I'm usually behind a camera, at a Pilates class, or finding the next museum or Broadway show worth the trip — and I've played piano since I was five.
